# Home page (POC overview)

Scope: `content/publicly-accessible-static-website/index.html`, the home page of the static-website POC, with `content/publicly-accessible-static-website/architecture.html` as its sibling. Visitor mode: Persuade, calm. Audience undecided (PRODUCT.md), so write for a general visitor or stakeholder. Scope is static websites only: no backend or document API.

Brief pins, all user-confirmed:
- A clean site, with https://g.ieffe.dev/ as the reference. It replaces the earlier sơn mài lacquer direction.
- The "Designed by flow" seal logo, Vietnamese-inspired line icons.
- Fonts: Texturina (primary) and Roboto (secondary).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A calm, white, line-drawn page in the manner of the reference. It has a bold/light two-weight headline, a line-art illustration, a row of three short columns and a hand-drawn pill call to action. The Vietnamese character lives in the seal, the icons and the illustration (nón lá, lantern), never in heavy decoration. It refuses dark panels, gradients, card grids and glow.

OWN-WORLD: White paper and ink #1c1c1c line work at one stroke weight. Cinnabar #b8321f is the single accent: the seal, the lantern, the numbered step dots, the A/B badges and the pill. Hairline #e6e4e0 rules separate sections. Texturina carries the display lines and Roboto the text. A system monospace appears only for the tagline and code.

STORY: Visitors grasp that this is one static pattern rebuilt per client. It is served at the edge, deployed through reviewed previews, and watched in every environment. They then read how a change goes live, pick a delivery option, and open the architecture page.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A slim header with the small seal and wordmark on the left and plain text nav on the right. The left column is the headline, "One static website," in 800 and "rebuilt for every client." in 400, then a mono tagline and a short lede. The right column is the line-art still life: a laptop with a simple page and a red seal, a nón lá leaning on it, and a red lantern swaying gently above. The three columns (Serve, Deploy, Watch) start just below the fold.

FORM: Reference-led (user-pinned), replacing seed 772fa771's sơn mài assignment at the user's request.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: the lantern sways slowly (off with reduced motion); the pill tilts on hover.
Build path: code-led (no image generation in this session).
